Collateral Card

A stock-backed loan can look healthy while its oracle price is frozen. As the stock token trades through the weekend, an agent can watch the gap and use its paid quote income to cut debt before the oracle updates.

In a Base mainnet fork replay, the loan survived with quote income. Removing that income led to liquidation.

Limits

The saved loan results came from a local Base mainnet fork with a replayed reopen. The page independently calculates rule outcomes from recorded prices.

The collateral token and price feed are stand-ins.

Whether an agent wallet can hold real NVDAc is unverified.

The x402 server and facilitator are local.

The 6 Nov guarded and control run repays from borrowed USDC.

All 518 quote sales came from one stand-in buyer. Customer demand and repayment economics remain untested. The fork supplied the gas.

The fork receipt has no repay time. The chart draws the repay when the forecast reads its last Sunday price.

The forecast band is in-sample, measured on the same recorded weekends.

The fork receipt and rule math differ slightly in the fourth decimal, so the two are labelled separately.
